Office Escape

Friday, 6:05 p.m. Your intern badge has expired. The night technician left a key at each workstation. Five doors stand between you and the weekend.

How to play

1. Type ls and press Enter to list files.

2. Read files to find a key to unlock the door.

3. Enter the key in the Door keypad. Open all five doors to escape.
